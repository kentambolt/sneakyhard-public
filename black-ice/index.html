Black Ice

One piece and a slippery surface. Where does the move end?

Play Black Ice online

Free in your browser. No account needed.

How to play

Use every tile exactly once. Ice never disappears, but when you step onto ice you keep sliding until you reach a normal tile, which you land on, or something you cannot enter.

How the levels were made

Black Ice boards are scored with the same model as One Path. A move is a press that reaches new tiles; sliding across ice in between is free. Small boards were searched exhaustively and larger ones with simulated annealing.

The ice has to earn its place. In every chosen board the solution slides across ice at least twice, otherwise it would just be One Path with decoration.

What makes it hard

Ice changes what a single move means. A short swipe can carry you across the board, so the obvious move often skips a tile you needed. Every board has exactly one solution.

Tips

  • Work out where each slide stops before you take it.
  • Ice can be crossed again and again. Use it as a corridor.

Made by search, tested by people

Every SneakyHard level is chosen by a search program that models how people solve puzzles: few pieces, simple rules and as much challenge as they can hold. We build these solvers together with AI and refine them through playtests.
